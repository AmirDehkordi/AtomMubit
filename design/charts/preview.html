Chart refinements for MuTick

Before and after for the KPI cards, the revenue chart and the compare view, in palette Ink. The figures are the live NVIDIA desk (Q3 FY2026 to Q2 FY2027). The layout and chart types stay the same, so Amir keeps a familiar dashboard. Only the marks, labels and colours change. Numbers like F3 refer to findings.md.

KPI cards

Same card, same order, same sparkline position.

Before · as shipped
After
  • F2Honest sparkline scale. Today each sparkline stretches its own min and max to fill the box, so gross margin's 1.6pp move from 73.4% to 75.0% looks like a cliff. After: money starts at zero, and margins sit in a fixed 10pp window around their average.
  • F3No change looks like no change. "+0.0pp" shown in green with an up arrow says "better". After: a grey chip with a dash.
  • F9Quieter line, clear end point. Flat 2px line with no gradient wash. The end dot is larger and has a ring in the card colour. No min or max dots: with four points they would add noise, not information.
  • F12Screen readers get the trend. The sparkline stays aria-hidden, and the card gains hidden text such as "up 17.9% vs Q1 FY2027, rising since Q3 FY2026 from $57.01B".

Revenue and net income

Same grouped bars, same legend position, same card. The tooltip is pinned on Q4 so you can compare it with the live app. Hover or tab through the quarters in the "after" chart.

Before · as shipped
After
  • F1Net income in a darker grey. Revenue blue and net-income grey are almost the same lightness: OKLab ΔE 14.0, below the 15 floor even for full colour vision. A chart-only grey #77716b brings it to ΔE 22.9, deutan 22.0, and 3.7:1 on the panel.
  • F4Clean axis. Ticks read $0, $25B, $50B, $75B, $100B, not "$100.00B". Gridlines are solid hairlines, not dashed. The zero line is one step stronger.
  • F5Label the latest quarter. Values sit on the newest pair only. Its axis label is bold, and the derived Q4 carries the same "D" as the table.
  • F6Flat bars, square at the base. No gradient, a 4px rounded top and a square base. Bars are 36px, not 56px, with a 4px gap. The legend uses squares to match the bars.
  • F7A tooltip that answers the next question. Each row shows its Q/Q change, and a footer gives net margin. Keys are short strokes and values lead.
  • F8The chart has a name. Recharts renders role="application" with no label. Add title and desc with a one-line summary. Arrow-key navigation already works.

Compare view

Same rows, monogram, value, Q/Q chip and "year earlier" line. NVDA and MSFT figures are the live desk.

Before · as shipped
After
  • F10Show the year-ago value on the bar. A thin tick on the same scale marks the year-earlier figure. The gap between the tick and the bar end is the growth, visible without reading the chip.
